Indian Cheese and Red Peppers
in Fragrant Spinach Sauce

Recipe by Julie Sahni, from Classic Indian Vegetarian and Grain Cooking (October 2004) · Epicurious · sent by Melissa, January 2012

Another Epicurious "send to a friend" from Melissa. Pan-fried paneer (or farmer's cheese, or tofu) and sweet red pepper strips folded into a fragrant puree of spinach, green pepper, onion, ginger, and garam masala — a rich, saucy curry built for rice.

Vegetariandiet
Indiancuisine
~45 minactive time

Ingredients

  • Indian cheese (paneer) made with 8 cups milk, or 8 oz farmer's cheese, or 2 cakes tofu, sliced as paneer
  • 2 cups cooked spinach
  • 1 sweet green pepper, cored and seeded
  • 6–8 tbsp light vegetable oil
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour, for dusting
  • 1½ cups finely chopped onion
  • 2 tbsp grated or crushed fresh ginger
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 4 hot green chilies, minced
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 tsp coarse salt, or to taste
  • 2 medium sweet red peppers, cored, seeded, and cut into 1-inch-wide strips
  • 2 tsp garam masala

Method

  1. Dry the cheese. Lay the paneer, farmer's cheese, or tofu pieces in a single layer on a paper-towel-lined cookie sheet and let dry 10 minutes, so they hold their shape while cooking.
  2. Puree. Puree the spinach and green pepper together in a food processor or blender until as fine and velvety as possible.
  3. Fry the cheese. Heat 4 tbsp oil in a large, heavy nonstick pan over medium heat. Dust the paneer pieces lightly with flour and fry in batches, turning and tossing, until light golden, 2–3 minutes per batch. Watch closely so they don't burn. Set aside on a plate.
  4. Build the sauce. Add 2 more tbsp oil to the pan with the onion. Fry, stirring constantly, until brown, about 20 minutes. Add the ginger and fry 2 minutes more. Add the turmeric and green chilies, stir a few seconds, then add the spinach puree, ½ cup water, salt, and red pepper strips. Bring to a boil, lower heat, and cook covered 2 minutes.
  5. Finish. Add the fried cheese, mix thoroughly, and cook 2 minutes more. Stir in the garam masala. Reheat before serving and, if desired, fold in the remaining 2 tbsp oil to glaze and mellow the sauce.

To make paneer from scratch: Bring 8 cups whole milk to a boil, add 3 tbsp lemon juice, and reduce heat. Once curds separate from the whey, pour through cheesecloth, rinse under cold water, twist to remove excess liquid, and hang to drain 1 hour. Weight it down for 30 minutes, then cut into cubes. Keep refrigerated in water up to 4 days.

Garam masala is a north Indian spice blend including cinnamon and cloves, available at Indian markets.
