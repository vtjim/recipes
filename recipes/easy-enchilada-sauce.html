Easy Enchilada Sauce

Recipe by Emeril Lagasse, Essence of Emeril · Food Network · sent by Melissa, October 2011

Melissa forwarded this one with no comment needed — a pantry-staple enchilada sauce built on a quick chili-powder roux, no roasting or blending required. Good to have on hand whenever enchiladas are the plan.

2½ cupsyield
~20 mintotal time
Saucecourse

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • ¼ cup chili powder
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 10 oz tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Make the roux. In a medium saucepan, heat the oil, then add the flour, smoothing and stirring with a wooden spoon. Cook 1 minute.
  2. Bloom the spices. Add the chili powder and cook 30 seconds more.
  3. Simmer. Add the stock, tomato paste, oregano, cumin, and salt. Stir to combine and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and cook 15 minutes, until the sauce thickens and smooths out.
