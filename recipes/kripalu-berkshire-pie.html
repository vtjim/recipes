Kripalu Berkshire Pie

Recipe from the Kripalu kitchen · sent by Melissa, December 2013

"What do you think?" was Melissa's whole pitch on this one. A hearty vegetarian pie from the Kripalu Center in the Berkshires — sautéed onion and cabbage folded with broccoli, cheddar, ricotta, sour cream, and grated carrot, baked in a whole wheat crust. Comfort food with a health-retreat pedigree.

6servings
Vegetariandiet
40 minbake time

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe Basic Pie Crust (see note)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cups onions, cubed (4–5 medium)
  • 1½ cups green cabbage, chopped
  • 2¼ tsp salt
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp dried tarragon
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 2½ cups small broccoli florets
  • 2 cups mild cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1½ cups ricotta cheese
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ¾ cup carrots, grated
  • ⅓ cup unbleached white flour

Method

  1. Make the crust. Mix the flours and salt, cut in cold butter, and add ice water gradually to form a ball. Chill 30 minutes, then roll to ⅛-inch thickness and fit into a 9-inch pie pan.
  2. Preheat. Set oven to 375°F.
  3. Cook the vegetables. Heat the oil and sauté the onions 3–5 minutes until translucent. Add the cabbage, salt, herbs, and pepper; sauté 5–10 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  4. Fill. Mix in the broccoli, both cheeses, sour cream, carrots, and flour. Pour into the prepared crust.
  5. Bake. Bake 40 minutes. Cool 5–10 minutes before serving.

Crust: Combine ½ cup whole wheat pastry flour, ½ cup unbleached white flour, and a pinch of salt. Cut in ⅓ cup cold cubed butter, then add ¼ cup ice water and 1 tsp maple syrup to bring together.
