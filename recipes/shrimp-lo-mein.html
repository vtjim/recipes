Shrimp Lo Mein

Recipe by Kelsey Nixon · Food Network · sent by Melissa, April 2015

Another "all-star, easy-to-follow" forward from Melissa. Egg noodles get tossed in a wok with quick-seared shrimp, mushrooms, carrot, celery, and Napa cabbage, all pulled together with an oyster sauce-Sriracha mixture thickened at the end with cornstarch. Faster than delivery.

4servings
35 mintotal time
20 minactive time

Ingredients

  • Salt, to taste
  • 8 oz dried Chinese egg noodles (or 1 lb fresh)
  • 1 packet chicken bouillon, dissolved in 1¾ cups hot water
  • ¼ cup plus 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha chili sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic, divided
  • 2 tsp minced ginger, divided
  • 1 cup thinly sliced white button mushrooms
  • 2 stalks celery, thinly sliced
  • 1 large carrot, shredded
  • 1 small bunch scallions, sliced, divided
  • 1 lb small shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • ¼ head Napa cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch

Method

  1. Cook the noodles. Boil in salted water per package directions; drain and set aside.
  2. Mix the sauce. Combine the bouillon, oyster sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, and Sriracha in a measuring cup.
  3. Cook the shrimp. Heat a wok over high heat. Add 1 tbsp oil with half the garlic, ginger, and scallions; sauté 30 seconds. Add the shrimp and cook until pink and curled, about 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  4. Cook the vegetables. Add the remaining oil, garlic, ginger, and scallions to the wok; sauté 30 seconds. Add the mushrooms, celery, carrot, and cabbage; cook 4–5 minutes until browning.
  5. Finish. Whisk the cornstarch into 2 tbsp cold water and stir into the sauce mixture. Pour into the wok and simmer, then toss in the shrimp and noodles. Serve immediately.
