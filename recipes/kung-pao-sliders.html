Kung Pao Sliders

Recipe by Mariano Gascon · Vegetarian Times · sent by Melissa, January 2014

Melissa's note was just "What do you think?" — hard to say no to a slider. This one won Best Appetizer in Vegetarian Times' 2011 Chefs' Challenge: soy-marinated tofu baked until crisp, tucked into slider buns with a peanut-sesame carrot-and-zucchini slaw.

16 slidersyield
Vegandiet
~85 mintotal time

Ingredients

  • 1 (14 oz) package extra-firm water-packed tofu, drained and patted dry
  • 4 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 16 slider-size buns
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (for the slaw)
  • 2 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp no-salt-added creamy peanut butter
  • 5 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil (for the slaw)
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ⅛ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper (optional)
  • 4 medium carrots, julienned (8 oz)
  • 2 medium zucchini, julienned (8 oz)
  • ¼ cup dry roasted peanuts, finely chopped

Method

  1. Marinate the tofu. Slice the tofu lengthwise into 8 pieces, then halve each to make 16 pieces. Arrange in a baking dish. Whisk together the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and cornstarch, and pour over the tofu. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour.
  2. Make the slaw. Whisk together the soy sauce, mayonnaise, peanut butter, sugar, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, pepper, and cayenne. Toss with the carrots, zucchini, and chopped peanuts. Chill.
  3. Bake the tofu. Preheat oven to 375°F and spray a baking sheet with cooking spray. Drain the tofu and arrange on the sheet. Bake 25 minutes, flip, then bake 20–25 minutes more until crispy.
  4. Assemble. Place one tofu slice and about 2 tbsp slaw in each bun.
