Baked Parmesan Tilapia

Recipe by Mike · Allrecipes · sent by Melissa, March 2013

Melissa's one-line pitch was "This sounds yummy," Jim's reply was "I saw and thought same thing. Tomorrow?", and Melissa's answer was "Sure" — dinner plans made in three texts. A ranch-dressing dip and a Parmesan-breadcrumb coat, baked instead of fried, so the crunch comes without the oil.

8servings
Weeknighttag
~35 mintotal time

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup prepared ranch dressing
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup dry bread crumbs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ tsp seasoned salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp celery salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp ground paprika
  • ½ tsp dried parsley
  • ¼ tsp dried basil
  • 8 (6 oz) tilapia fillets

Method

  1. Preheat. Set oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with foil and coat with cooking spray.
  2. Set up the dredge. Whisk the milk and ranch dressing together in one shallow bowl. Put the flour in a second bowl. In a third, combine the bread crumbs, Parmesan, seasoned salt, pepper, celery salt, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, parsley, and basil.
  3. Bread the fish. Dredge each fillet in flour, dip in the milk-ranch mixture, then press into the bread crumb mixture to coat both sides.
  4. Bake. Arrange the fillets on the prepared sheet and lightly mist the tops with cooking spray. Bake 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the fish flakes easily with a fork.
