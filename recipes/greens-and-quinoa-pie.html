Greens and Quinoa Pie

Recipe by Renee Cornett · Vegetarian Times · sent by Melissa, April 2014

A crustless pie held together with egg and quinoa instead of pastry — swiss chard, caramelized onions, feta, and dill baked into a savory wedge you can eat warm or at room temperature. Good with a dill or mint yogurt sauce on the side.

Vegetariandiet
9-inch pieyield
~1 hrtotal time

Ingredients

  • ½ cup quinoa, rinsed and drained
  • 1 large bunch swiss chard (1 to 1¼ lb), cut into bite-sized pieces, stems reserved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 medium onions, thinly sliced (1 cup)
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced (¼ cup)
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh dill
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 3 eggs, lightly beaten
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the quinoa. Combine the rinsed quinoa with 1 cup water and salt, if desired, in a covered saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce to medium-low, and simmer 15 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl.
  2. Prep the chard. Steam the chard leaves until wilted, then cool, squeeze out excess moisture, chop, and stir into the quinoa.
  3. Sauté. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté the onions, the white part of the green onions, and the chopped chard stems for 10 minutes, until browned.
  4. Combine. Add the onion mixture, green onion tops, dill, and feta to the quinoa. Stir in the eggs and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Bake, part one. Pour 1 tbsp oil into a 9-inch pie pan and heat in a 350°F oven for 5 minutes. Swirl to coat, spread the quinoa mixture in the pan, and bake 20 minutes.
  6. Bake, part two. Drizzle the remaining 1 tbsp oil over the pie and bake 20–30 minutes more, until golden brown. Cool 15 minutes before slicing.

Serve with a dill or mint yogurt sauce alongside.
