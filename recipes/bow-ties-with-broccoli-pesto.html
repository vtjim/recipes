Bow Ties with Broccoli Pesto

Recipe from Vegetarian Times · sent by Melissa, April 2014

A green pesto built from broccoli instead of basil — toasted hazelnuts, parsley, mint, lemon, and garlic get pulsed with blanched broccoli into something brighter and lighter than the usual jar. Tossed with whole-wheat farfalle for a quick vegetarian dinner.

Vegetariandiet
~25 minactive time
Pastacourse

Ingredients

  • 12 oz farfalle (bow tie) pasta, preferably whole wheat
  • 6 tbsp hazelnuts, chopped
  • 2 cups broccoli, chopped
  • 1½ cups loosely packed parsley leaves
  • ¼ cup olive oil, plus 2 tbsp extra for drizzling
  • ½ cup mint leaves
  • 4 tsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp lemon zest
  • 1½ tsp capers, rinsed and drained (optional)
  • 5 large garlic cloves
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Cook the pasta. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the farfalle according to package directions. Drain, reserving a little pasta water.
  2. Toast the hazelnuts. In a dry skillet over medium heat, toast the hazelnuts until fragrant, a few minutes. Set aside.
  3. Blanch the broccoli. Cook the broccoli briefly in boiling water until just bright green and tender-crisp, then drain.
  4. Make the pesto. In a food processor, pulse the hazelnuts, broccoli, parsley, olive oil, mint, lemon juice, lemon zest, capers, and garlic until it forms a coarse pesto. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Toss and serve. Toss the pesto with the hot pasta, loosening with reserved pasta water if needed. Drizzle with the remaining olive oil.
