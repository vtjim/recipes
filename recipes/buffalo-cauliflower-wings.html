Buffalo Cauliflower Wings

Jeanine Donofrio & Phoebe Moore · Love and Lemons

An irresistible side dish or game-day snack: cauliflower florets get dipped in a cornstarch-thickened batter, baked until the edges crisp up, then brushed twice with buffalo sauce for maximum tang without ever touching a fryer.

4servings
15 minprep time
35 mincook time
Vegandiet

Ingredients

  • ⅔ cup all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ¾ cup water
  • 1 cauliflower (about 2 lbs), broken into large florets
  • Buffalo sauce, for brushing
  • Ranch, vegan ranch, or blue cheese dressing, optional, for dipping

Method

  1. Preheat. Preheat the oven to 450°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Batter the cauliflower. Whisk together the flour, cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and salt in a large bowl. Add the water and whisk until smooth. Coat the cauliflower florets in the batter.
  3. First bake. Arrange the florets in a single layer on the prepared sheets. Bake 10 minutes, carefully flip, then bake 10 more minutes, swapping the pans' positions.
  4. Sauce and finish. Brush with buffalo sauce and bake 12–15 minutes more, until dark and crispy around the edges.
  5. Serve. Remove and brush with additional sauce. Serve immediately with dipping sauce, if desired.

Don't skip the parchment paper — the batter sticks without it.

Brush sauce twice: mid-bake and again just before serving.

Best eaten immediately while warm and crispy. Leftovers keep airtight up to 3 days but lose their crunch.

Gluten-free: substitute a 1:1 gluten-free all-purpose flour blend.
