I Can't Believe It's Not Chicken
(Super-Savory Grated Tofu)

Recipe by Nisha Vora, adapted from Big Vegan Flavor (Avery, 2024) · NYT Cooking

Grating super-firm tofu on a box grater turns it into something shockingly meat-like — no pressing required. Pan-fried until golden and tossed in a sauce built from tamari, Chinese black vinegar, gochugaru, and toasted sesame oil, it's been called "spicy ground chicken" and "larb-esque." Good over rice for dinner, just as good cold for breakfast the next day.

4servings
~25 minactive time
Vegandiet
~3 cupsyield

Ingredients

  • 12 oz super-firm tofu (vacuum-packed)
  • 1½ tbsp neutral oil (canola or grapeseed)
  • 4 scallions, sliced on an angle — dark green tops reserved for garnish
  • 1–2 Thai bird's eye chiles, thinly sliced (optional)
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp Chinkiang (Chinese black) vinegar
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chile flakes)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar (or maple syrup / agave)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Method

  1. Grate the tofu. Wrap it in a thin dish towel, gently squeeze to remove some water — don't squish it — then grate on the large holes of a box grater. Slice any crumbled bits thin.
  2. Sauté the aromatics. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add the scallion whites, chiles, and garlic; cook about 2 minutes, until the garlic is lightly golden.
  3. Cook the tofu. Add the grated tofu and toss to coat in the oil. Cook undisturbed for 2 minutes, then stir every 2 minutes until golden in spots, 10–14 minutes total.
  4. Make the sauce. Whisk together the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, gochugaru, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  5. Sauce it. Pour the sauce into the pan — it will bubble rapidly. Stir to coat evenly and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant.
  6. Serve. Sprinkle with sesame seeds. Serve hot over rice, topped with the reserved scallion greens and cilantro.

No super-firm tofu? Use extra-firm, press for 10 minutes, grate, then blot with towels to remove water.

Gluten-free: use tamari, and swap the Chinkiang vinegar for 2 tsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp balsamic.

Keeps 4–5 days refrigerated. About 219 calories per ¾-cup serving.
