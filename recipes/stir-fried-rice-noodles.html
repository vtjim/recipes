Stir-Fried Rice Noodles

Recipe from Vegetarian Times · sent by Melissa, April 2012

Melissa's note: "Looks good, right. I liked your taco pizza recipe. We should pick something different out to make this weekend." After a brief soak, rice noodles get tossed into a fast stir-fry with asparagus, snow peas, and scrambled egg, brought together with a soy-Sriracha broth. Skip the eggs to make it vegan.

4servings
Vegetariandiet
~25 mintotal time

Ingredients

  • 6 oz rice sticks or rice vermicelli
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, divided
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ lb asparagus, trimmed and sliced diagonally into 1-inch pieces (2 cups)
  • 7 oz snow peas, trimmed and sliced lengthwise into ½-inch-wide strips (2½ cups)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (1 tbsp)
  • ¾ cup low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sriracha sauce
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced (⅓ cup)

Method

  1. Soak the noodles. Soak the rice sticks in hot water per package directions until pliable, then drain.
  2. Scramble the eggs. Heat 1 tsp sesame oil in a wok or large skillet. Beat the eggs with the white pepper, scramble until just set, and set aside.
  3. Stir-fry. Heat the remaining sesame oil in the wok. Add the asparagus and snow peas; stir-fry 3–4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Add the sauce. Whisk together the broth, soy sauce, and Sriracha. Add to the pan and bring to a simmer.
  5. Finish. Stir in the drained rice sticks and cook 3–5 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed. Fold in the scrambled egg and green onions; serve hot.
