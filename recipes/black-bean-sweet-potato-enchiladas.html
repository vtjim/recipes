Black Bean & Sweet Potato Enchiladas

Robyn Conley Downs · Real Food Whole Life

A one-pan, make-ahead casserole. Sweet potatoes roast right in the baking dish with cumin, garlic, and chili powder, then get mashed into a filling of black beans, cream cheese, Mexican-blend cheese, and green chilis. The same dish then holds the sauce-dipped, rolled corn tortillas for the final bake under a golden layer of cheese.

Ingredients

Sweet potatoes

  • 3 cups sweet potato, peeled and diced into 1-inch pieces (about 2 large)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp granulated garlic powder
  • 1 tsp mild chili powder

Filling

  • 1 (15-oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 4 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend, divided
  • 1 (4-oz) can diced green chilis

Sauce & assembly

  • 1½ cups red enchilada sauce, divided (homemade, or one can)
  • 12 corn tortillas, gently warmed

Equipment: one 10 × 7-inch baking dish.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. In the baking dish, toss the diced sweet potatoes with the olive oil, ¼ tsp salt, cumin, garlic powder, and chili powder until coated.
  3. Cover tightly with foil and bake 30–40 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are very tender. Remove the foil and save it for later.
  4. While the potatoes bake, combine the black beans, cream cheese, ½ cup cheese, green chilis, and ¼ cup enchilada sauce in a large bowl. Lightly mash about half the beans, then stir until well combined.
  5. Transfer the cooked sweet potatoes to the bowl, scraping in any bits left in the pan. Mash them, then stir until everything is combined.
  6. Pour ¾ cup enchilada sauce into the same baking dish. Dip both sides of each tortilla in the sauce and stack them on a plate.
  7. If the pan looks dry after dipping, add another ¼ cup enchilada sauce to the bottom.
  8. Fill each dipped tortilla with about 3 tbsp of filling, roll tightly, and nestle it into the pan.
  9. Spread the remaining ¼ cup enchilada sauce over the top, then sprinkle with the remaining ½ cup cheese.
  10. Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes, until bubbling around the edges.
  11. Remove the foil and bake 15 minutes more, until the cheese is golden brown.
  12. Let rest 5 minutes before serving.

Warming tortillas: wrap the stack of 12 in a damp towel and microwave 30–45 seconds to reduce cracking.

Spicier: use hot diced green chilis and hot chili powder instead of mild.

Gluten-free: use gluten-free enchilada sauce and tortillas.

Dairy-free: swap in dairy-free cheese shreds and cream cheese.

Beans: kidney or pinto beans can stand in for black beans.

Make ahead: assemble up to 3 days ahead and refrigerate. Add a few minutes to the bake, or let the dish sit at room temperature about 15 minutes first.

Homemade sauce: the Easy Enchilada Sauce already in the Recipe Box works here.
