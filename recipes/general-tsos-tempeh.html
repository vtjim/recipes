General Tso's Tempeh

Tasty · scanned recipe card

Chicken-nugget-sized pieces of tempeh (or seitan, per the card's own substitution notes) marinate in cornstarch, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, rice wine, and soy sauce, then get pan-fried and tossed in a sweet-savory sauce built on tomato paste, rice vinegar, and honey. The card's history shows it made at least twice — once with seitan and steamed broccoli, once weighing a tempeh-vs-seitan swap.

4servings
1 hr+chill time
Vegandiet

Ingredients

  • 16 oz tempeh (or 8 oz seitan — see notes)
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced, divided
  • 4 tsp ginger, grated, divided
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil, divided
  • 2 tbsp rice wine
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce, divided
  • ¼ cup oil
  • ½ tsp red pepper flake
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • ¼ cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Sesame seed, optional, to garnish

Cornstarch slurry

  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Chop the tempeh into chicken-nugget-sized pieces and transfer to a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the cornstarch, paprika, ginger, garlic, sesame oil, rice wine, and soy sauce to the bowl. Mix until incorporated. Cover and chill in the fridge for 1 hour minimum.
  3. Add oil to a pan on medium-high heat and pan-fry the tempeh until browned on both sides. Remove from pan.
  4. In the same pan, add in oil and sesame oil on medium-high heat.
  5. Add the garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes, and cook until golden brown and aromatic, about 1 minute.
  6. Add the tomato paste and vegetable broth, and stir until incorporated.
  7. Add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, and honey, and stir until all are incorporated.
  8. Add the cornstarch slurry (mix the cornstarch with the water in a small bowl until a paste forms) into the sauce mixture and stir until the sauce thickens.
  9. Return the tempeh to the pan and allow it to incorporate with the sauce. Top with sesame seeds, optional.
  10. Enjoy!

From the card, 1/24/19: "Yum!" — made with 16 oz seitan and ½ steamed broccoli added in. Reminder to fry, and chill in the fridge 1 hour (per step 2).

From the card, 11/2/21: a note weighing 16 oz tempeh against 8 oz seitan as the swap.
